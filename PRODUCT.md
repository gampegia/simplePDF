# Product

## Register

product

## Users

SimplePDF is for Mac power users who keep a small working set of PDFs open at once, usually two or three documents across tabs or separate windows. They value fast opening, predictable navigation, presentation mode, document metadata, and a low-friction path toward future editing features.

## Product Purpose

SimplePDF exists to be a focused, modern PDF workspace for macOS: lighter than Acrobat, more deliberate than a bare viewer, and organized enough for multi-document workflows. Success means users can open, compare, search, present, and eventually edit PDFs without the interface getting in the way.

## Brand Personality

Modern, precise, independent. The app should feel native enough to be trusted on macOS, but not anonymous. It can carry a recognizable SimplePDF identity through accent color, spacing, icon treatment, and empty states while keeping the document itself visually dominant.

## Anti-references

Avoid heavy Adobe Acrobat density, overdecorated card layouts, decorative glassmorphism, loud multicolor palettes, generic AI-looking gradients, and UI chrome that competes with the PDF. Avoid making standard macOS controls feel unfamiliar just for personality.

## Design Principles

- Document first: the PDF page remains the primary visual object.
- Branded restraint: SimplePDF can have a clear accent and visual rhythm, but accent color is reserved for selection, primary action, and state.
- Workspace clarity: tabs, windows, sidebar, status, and overlays should support small multi-document workflows without visual noise.
- Future-editable foundation: layout, color, typography, and surface choices must be tokenized so annotation and editing tools can be added cleanly.
- Native familiarity: use macOS interaction patterns where they help users trust the app.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for text and controls. Maintain keyboard access for search, page jump, document open/close, presentation mode, and settings. Respect reduced-motion expectations by keeping product motion short and state-driven. Theme work must preserve contrast in light and dark appearances and support future color-blind-safe accent choices.
